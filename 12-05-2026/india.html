India

India is one of the world’s oldest civilizations and the largest democracy by population. Located in South Asia, it is bordered by Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, and Myanmar, while the Indian Ocean lies to its south. India is known for its rich cultural heritage, diverse traditions, and significant contributions to science, mathematics, philosophy, and art. The history of India dates back thousands of years to the ancient Indus Valley Civilization, one of the earliest urban civilizations in the world. Over centuries, India witnessed the rise and fall of powerful kingdoms and empires such as the Maurya Empire, Gupta Empire, Mughal Empire, and later British colonial rule. After a long struggle for independence led by figures like Mahatma Gandhi, India gained freedom from British rule on August 15, 1947. India is a land of incredible diversity. It has 28 states and several union territories, each with its own language, customs, cuisine, and festivals. The Constitution of India recognizes multiple official languages, with Hindi and English widely used for communication and administration. Religions such as Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Buddhism, and Jainism are practiced across the country, making India a symbol of unity in diversity. The country is also famous for its geographical variety. From the snow-covered Himalayan Mountains in the north to the tropical beaches of Goa and Kerala in the south, India offers a wide range of landscapes. Major rivers such as the Ganga, Yamuna, and Brahmaputra play an important role in agriculture and daily life.

ISRO

Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)is the national space agency of India. It was established in 1969 by the visionary scientist Vikram Sarabhai, who is known as the father of the Indian space program. ISRO is headquartered in Bengaluru and operates under the Department of Space of the Government of India. The main objective of ISRO is to develop space technology and use it for national development. Over the years, ISRO has achieved remarkable success in satellite communication, weather forecasting, remote sensing, navigation, and scientific research. It has launched numerous satellites that help in agriculture, disaster management, education, and telecommunications. ISRO gained international recognition through successful missions such as Chandrayaan-1, which discovered evidence of water molecules on the Moon, and Mangalyaan, India’s first mission to Mars. In 2023, Chandrayaan-3 successfully landed near the Moon’s south pole, making India the first country to achieve this milestone. Known for cost-effective and reliable missions, ISRO has become one of the world’s leading space agencies and a symbol of India’s scientific progress and innovation.
