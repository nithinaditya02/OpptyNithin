Fest Events

Technical Events

  1. Hackathon

    2. The Hackathon conducted as part of the ABC College Fest was one of the most exciting and innovative events of the celebration. It brought together talented students from different departments to showcase their creativity, technical knowledge, and problem-solving abilities. Participants worked in teams to develop unique solutions for real-world challenges within a limited time. The event encouraged collaboration, critical thinking, and teamwork among students. Mentors and judges guided the participants and evaluated their projects based on originality, practicality, and presentation. The atmosphere was energetic and competitive, motivating students to give their best performance. Various innovative ideas related to technology, social impact, and smart solutions were presented during the hackathon. Winners were awarded prizes and certificates for their outstanding efforts. Overall, the hackathon became a great learning experience for all participants and highlighted the importance of innovation and technology in today’s world. It was truly a memorable part of the ABC College Fest.

  2. Robotics Challenge

    3. The Robotics Challenge organized during the ABC College Fest was one of the most thrilling and innovative events of the program. The competition attracted enthusiastic students from various departments who showcased their technical skills, creativity, and teamwork through exciting robotic projects. Participants designed and programmed robots to perform specific tasks such as obstacle navigation, object detection, line following, and automated movements. The event provided students with an opportunity to apply their theoretical knowledge in practical situations and explore the latest advancements in robotics and automation. The competition was conducted in multiple rounds, where teams were judged based on design, efficiency, accuracy, and problem-solving ability. Faculty members and industry experts evaluated the performances and guided students with valuable suggestions and technical insights. The atmosphere throughout the event was energetic and inspiring, encouraging innovation and healthy competition among participants. The Robotics Challenge not only enhanced students’ technical knowledge but also improved their confidence, communication, and collaborative skills. Winners received prizes and certificates in recognition of their outstanding performances. Overall, the event became a memorable highlight of the ABC College Fest and inspired students to explore future opportunities in robotics and technology.

Non Technical Events

  • Dance Competition

    • The Dance Competition held during the ABC College Fest was a vibrant and energetic event that added color and excitement to the celebration. Students from various departments participated enthusiastically, showcasing their talent in different dance styles such as classical, hip-hop, freestyle, folk, and contemporary. Each performance reflected creativity, dedication, and hard work, making the event highly entertaining for the audience. The stage was filled with rhythm, energy, and expression as participants performed with confidence and passion. Music, costumes, and choreography came together beautifully, creating a lively atmosphere in the auditorium. The judges evaluated the performances based on coordination, expression, originality, timing, and overall presentation. The audience cheered loudly for every performance, encouraging the participants and boosting their confidence. The competition not only highlighted the artistic talents of students but also promoted cultural unity and teamwork. Winners were awarded prizes and certificates for their excellent performances. Overall, the dance competition became one of the most memorable and enjoyable events of the ABC college

  • Music Concert

    • The Music Concert organized during the ABC College Fest was one of the most entertaining and unforgettable events of the celebration. It brought together talented student musicians and singers who captivated the audience with their soulful and energetic performances. The concert featured a variety of musical styles, including classical, Bollywood, rock, and fusion, creating a lively and engaging atmosphere for everyone present. Students performed both solo and group acts, showcasing their vocal skills and instrumental talent with great confidence. The stage was beautifully set with lights and sound effects that enhanced the overall experience. The audience thoroughly enjoyed each performance, cheering and applauding the artists throughout the event. The judges evaluated the participants based on voice quality, rhythm, coordination, stage presence, and creativity. The event encouraged students to express their passion for music and build confidence in performing before a large crowd. Overall, the music concert was a grand success and became one of the most memorable highlights of the ABC College Fest.

  • Photography Contest

    • The Photography Competition held during the ABC College Fest was a creative and visually inspiring event that attracted many talented students. Participants showcased their photography skills by capturing unique moments, emotions, nature scenes, cultural themes, and everyday life from innovative perspectives. The competition encouraged students to observe their surroundings more closely and express stories through images. Students submitted both digital and printed photographs, each reflecting originality, composition, lighting, and creativity. The event highlighted different categories such as nature photography, portrait photography, street photography, and abstract art. The judging panel, consisting of faculty members and professional photographers, evaluated entries based on clarity, creativity, technical quality, and storytelling ability. The exhibition of photographs created a beautiful display for visitors during the fest, drawing appreciation from students and staff alike. Many participants expressed that the competition helped them improve their photography skills and boosted their confidence in visual arts. Overall, the photography competition was a highly engaging and artistic part of the ABC College Fest, celebrating creativity and imagination through the lens.
